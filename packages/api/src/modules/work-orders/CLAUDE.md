# Work Orders module (`packages/api/src/modules/work-orders`)

Phase 2 W2, ported from Majimap. Moved unchanged out of `packages/api/CLAUDE.md`.

`createWorkOrdersRouter(work)` mounted at `workOrders`, built over a `WorkRegistry` so tests bring their own kinds and resolvers (`test/harness.ts` stands in for GIS so these suites don't depend on its engine). Procedures come from `workOrderProcedures(work)` (`procedures.ts`): `base` (Work Orders or GIS licensed; any work-order permission; adds `ctx.level`, `ctx.licensed`, `ctx.abilities`, `ctx.visibility`), `acting` (`work_orders:execute` or `assign`) and `full` (level full, NOT_FOUND otherwise: crews, `schedule`, SLA). Permissions replace Majimap's roles: execute is "act", assign is "manage" (raise for anyone, act on any visible job, link anyone's logs, write any job branch), `review` approves, returns and finalizes, `cancel` cancels; `work_orders:read` sees every job in scope, otherwise only one's own and one's crews' (`access.ts`, pure). The state machine (`transitions.ts`) and `planCreate` (`rules.ts`) are pure and verbatim; device actions take a client-minted `eventId`, `occurredAt` and `position`, go through `lockAndReplay` and `settle` (`store.ts`): outcomes applied, unchanged, replayed, rejected, stale; an id used elsewhere (other job, person, action or organization) is CONFLICT. Every applied change is audited (`work_orders.<event type>`) and a status move publishes `work_order.status_changed`; also `work_order.created`, `.assigned`, `.completed` (with the capture in force) and the kind's own `completionEvent` with the same payload (`events.ts`). Finishing checks the kind's `evidence` (photos on the job, GPS on the event) and `capture` schema; failures are rejected outcomes, the capture is appended to `work_order_capture`. `workOrders.kinds` also returns each kind's `captureSchema` (the capture form as JSON Schema, null when none) for clients that draw the form. Review and SLA hours per kind are overridable with the `work-orders.kind_policy` setting (`policy.ts`), read at creation. `sweepOverdueWorkOrders(db)` (`sla.ts`) runs in the worker and raises `WORK_ORDER_OVERDUE` once per job (resolved on completion, cancel or a reschedule into the future). `index.ts` exports `register(work)` (kinds `repair`, `inspection_general`, `survey`, the `wo-` branch guard and annotator, the asset-work lookup), called in `wiring.ts`. Multi-level review through approvals (K-05) is not wired yet.
