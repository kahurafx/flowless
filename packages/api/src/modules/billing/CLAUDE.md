# Billing module (`packages/api/src/modules/billing`)

Phase 5 P5-3 (billing cycles: B-01 to B-04, B-15, B-16), P5-4 (billing runs: B-01, B-05 to B-10, B-12, NF-11, NF-24) and P5-5 (disconnection, corrections, revenue cases: B-09, B-12 to B-14, B-19) are described in their own sections at the end; the rest is Phase 4.

Phase 4 P4-3 and P4-4: B-05, B-06, B-08 to B-12, B-18, C-04, C-06, C-08, C-11, T-03, T-04, NF-22. Billing maps the pure `@flowless/billing-engine` onto the `billing` schema (append-only documents and double-entry ledger, see `packages/db/CLAUDE.md`). It reads other modules only through their `index.ts` (Tariffs' `categoryPricing`, `tariffVersionInForce`, `chargeItemAmountInForce`, `allocateInvoiceNumber`) and never touches their tables.

## Procedures
`billingRouter` mounted at `billing`, from `procedures.ts`: `read` (`billing:read`), `issue` (`billing:run`), `adjust` (`billing:adjust`).
- `invoices.issueUsage` (issue): price a reading or estimate for an account and period, post the invoice (or credit note when the total is negative) and its ledger transaction, or hold it. `invoices.issueFinal` (issue, `final.ts`): the final bill on disconnection (B-12): the engine's `finalBill` (final units, the month's standing charge and the turn-off fee, which is the amount in force of `turnOffFeeChargeItemId`, cited on the fee line), posted as an invoice (or credit note) with `document.final_bill = true`; once per `eventKey` (idempotency key `final:<eventKey>`, a repeat returns the first, another account under it is CONFLICT); whatever the status table says, it is not held. With no earlier reading the dial only sets the baseline and no bill is made.
- `adjustments.request` (adjust) asks the K-05 approval `billing.adjustment` (default one level `billing:approve`, `approvals.billing.adjustment`); `creditNotes.issue`, `debitNotes.issue` (adjust) cite the approved request (`approvalId`: same account, kind and amount; used once, the idempotency key is `note:<approvalId>`).
- `documents.void`, `documents.rebill` (adjust: permission, reason and audit, no approval); reads `documents.get`, `documents.list`, `balance`, `statement`.
Every change audits (`billing.<kind>_issued`, `billing.document_voided`, `billing.bill_held`) and publishes `billing.document_issued`, `.document_reversed`, `.bill_held` in the same transaction.

## Files
`usage.ts` (input schema, `prepareUsage` which builds the engine account and prices without writing, `issueUsage`, `holdBill`), `mapping.ts` (pure: tariff months to engine tariffs, engine lines to rows, stored rows to engine docs), `post.ts` (the only writer of documents and ledger entries: number, document, lines, balanced entries, or the mirror of a reversed transaction), `reversal.ts` (void and re-bill), `notes.ts`, `reads.ts` (derived reads), `settings.ts` (`billing.policy`: tier scaling, estimate average, fallback, outlier ceiling, dial digits), `approvals.ts`, `events.ts`, `final.ts`, `ports.ts` (Billing's side of the Accounts and Tariffs ports, registered by `registerKernel` through `registerPorts()`), `scope.ts` (K-03 checks against Accounts' published reads).

## Rules in force
- Postings for one account take turns (`lockAccount`, an advisory transaction lock); an idempotency key replays the earlier document, any other request under it is CONFLICT. A ledger transaction's key is `<document key>:ledger`.
- One ledger pair per non-zero line: receivable against revenue (deposit line: `deposit_liability`, transfer: `transfer_clearing`), sides swapped for negative lines; a document with only zero lines posts no ledger transaction. A reversal mirrors the original entries (swapped sides, `reverses_transaction_id`).
- T-04: usage and true-up lines cite `tariff_version_id` (the first when a span crosses versions; all are in `calculation`), standing lines cite `charge_item_id` and `charge_item_amount_id`. Meter rent is added to the standing line's amount and its amount id is in `calculation.chargeItemAmountIds`.
- A void is refused for a reversal, an already voided document, or an invoice with live notes against it. A re-bill prices the replacement as if the original were void; if the engine holds it, nothing is voided. Its pair keys are `<key>:void` and `<key>:rebill`.
- A held bill (engine `BillException`) raises an exception (`UNBILLED_ACCOUNT`, `NO_BASELINE` for that code) owned by the person who ran it, deduped per account, period and code; age is derived from `raised_at`.

## Ports and Accounts/Tariffs integration (P4-4)
All registered in `ports.ts`; the three Accounts ports carry `requires: ["billing"]`, so an organization without Billing is never asked (it terminates with `acknowledgeNoLedger`, activates by checklist, and succession stays `pending_ledger`).
- Termination check (C-11): `{ finalBillIssued, balance }`: a live (not reversed) `final_bill` document exists, and the receivable balance from the ledger. A voided final bill no longer counts.
- Gate source `payment_threshold` (C-06): `connectionPosition` = invoiced (receivable postings of charge, credit, adjustment and deposit transactions, reversals netting out) against paid (receivable postings of payment transactions and their reversals); met when paid covers the configured percent (rounded up) or fixed amount of invoiced; unmet with "No connection invoice has been issued" while nothing is invoiced. The account's whole invoiced total stands for "the connection invoice": a NEW or PENDING_PAYMENT account is not charged usage, so it holds only one-off documents. A gate decided by a source cannot be ticked by hand.
- Balance transfer (C-08): `balanceTransfer` posts one sealed `transfer` ledger transaction of four entries (old account receivable against `transfer_clearing`, new account's the other way; a credit moves the other way), `source_ref` and key `transfer:<commandId>`, so a replay posts nothing; a zero balance posts nothing; both accounts are locked in id order.
- Closed period (T-03): `closedPeriodSource` = latest `period_to` of documents that are not reversals and not reversed; Tariffs refuses a change dated on or before it.
- `chargesApplying` (C-04): `prepareUsage` asks Accounts' `chargesApplyingTo(tx, accountId)`. Consumption not billed holds the bill as STATUS_BLOCKED (exception `UNBILLED_ACCOUNT`); recurring not applying zeroes the standing line (it still cites the charge amount, T-04). An account Accounts does not know is billed as active (Billing can be exercised on opaque ids). Credit and debit notes, voids and final bills ignore the status table (corrections must stay possible).
- Scope (K-03): every procedure has `ctx.viewer`; `scope.ts` calls `accountVisibleTo` / `visibleAccountIds` (Accounts' index) so a limited member gets NOT_FOUND for an account, document or statement outside their areas and routes, and `documents.list` without an account is filtered to the visible ones.
- Applications (C-05): Accounts publishes `recordApplicationInvoiced` / `recordApplicationPaid` (the office's `markInvoiced` / `markPaid` as the system, replay by id). Billing does NOT yet consume `accounts.application_stage_changed` to issue the one-off connection invoice: an application has no account until `convert`, documents belong to an account, and the membership class and parent are not on a published read. Gap for Phase 6/7: the office still gives the references by hand.

## Assumptions until Phase 5
- No Metering: the caller passes the reading history (`readings`, the latest actual before the period is the baseline), the category and the invoice type. Flat-rate tariffs, discounts, quarterly or annual recurrence and late-reading corrections are not priced yet.
- `recheckActivation` on payment is Phase 6.

## Billing cycles (P5-3: B-01 to B-04, B-15, B-16)
Files: `cycles.ts` (open, advance, close, outcomes, reads, review), `cycle-machine.ts` (pure state table), `completeness.ts` (pure B-04 check), `cycle-review.ts` (pure buckets and sample), `period-lock.ts`, `cycle-settings.ts`, `cycle-events.ts`. Schema (migrations `0042`, `0043`): `cycle`, `cycle_event`, `cycle_account`, `cycle_outcome`, `cycle_review`; see `packages/db/CLAUDE.md`.

### Procedures (`billing.cycles.*`)
`open` (billing:run), `list`, `get`, `accounts` (paged, filter `outcome` incl. `none`, `expected`; scoped viewers see only visible accounts), `completeness`, `review.queue` (read); `advance`, `close`, `recordOutcome`, `review.bulkApprove`, `review.decide` (billing:run). All commands take client-minted ids and replay. `open` is idempotent by id and by route + period (a different id for the same route and period is CONFLICT).

### Rules in force
- One cycle per route and period (`period` YYYY-MM, `cycle_date` defaults to the period's last day, `frequency` monthly/bimonthly/quarterly from the `billing.cycle` setting or the input, `trigger` copied from the setting). State order: open, reading_window, validation, estimation, calculation, review, issue, deliver, completeness, closed. `advance` goes one step; skipping forward and going back (never to open) need a `reason`; `closed` is reached only by `close`, from `completeness`, and never left. The database enforces the same table (`guard_cycle_event` chains events by `seq`/`from_state`; `guard_cycle` lets `cycle.state` change only to the newest event's `to_state`). Entering `estimation` or `calculation` queues the per-account jobs (P5-4).
- B-03: on open the cycle takes Metering's snapshot (`takeSnapshot` for the route and cycle date, returns the existing one if the reading tasks already took it, so both use the same `snapshot_id`) and stores one `cycle_account` per account (expected when any of its service points is eligible; the snapshot reason is kept). Metering unlicensed: **fallback**, not refusal. Billing does not depend on Metering (`MODULE_DEPENDENCIES`), so the accounts come from Accounts' `routeServicePointFacts` through the pure `eligibilityOf(fact, hasMeter = true)`: live service point, consumption billed under C-04, and every such account is taken as eligible because there is no meter register to ask. `snapshot_id` is null and `cycles.get` says `eligibilitySource: accounts_fallback`.
- B-04: an outcome is one insert-only `cycle_outcome` row (`billed`/`billed_estimate` cite a document of that account and keep its total; `exception` and `excluded` need a reason; `exception` raises an `UNBILLED_ACCOUNT` queue item owned by the actor, dedupe `cycle:<cycle>:<account>`, linked by `exception_id`; a later non-exception outcome resolves it). The newest row per account is current. Only expected accounts take outcomes. `close` is PRECONDITION_FAILED while any expected account has no outcome, with counts and up to ten examples in the message (`completeness` returns the same data); the database function `billing.cycle_unexplained` refuses the close event too.
- B-15: a closed cycle locks its period for the accounts it covered (the months of its frequency ending at `period`). `issueUsage` (after its idempotent replay) calls `assertPeriodOpen`, PRECONDITION_FAILED with a pointer to credit/debit notes and void + re-bill (B-14); notes, voids, re-bills and final bills are not blocked. The database refuses new accounts, outcomes or reviews in a closed cycle. `closedPeriodSource` (Tariffs, T-03) now returns the later of the latest billed `period_to` and the last day of the latest closed cycle's period. Corrections into a locked period are P5-5 (below).
- B-01: settings `billing.cycle` (`trigger` batch | on_reading, `frequency`) are read at open; the trigger is acted on by `billing.bill_on_reading` (P5-4 below).
- B-16 (minimal): setting `billing.review` (`bulkApproveBelow`, `individualAbove` as digit strings of minor units, `sampleRate`, `sampleMinimum`). A billed outcome is `clean` (not flagged and at or under the bulk threshold), `attention` (flagged or over it) or `high` (over `individualAbove`; deciding needs `billing:approve`). `review.bulkApprove` (review state only) approves every clean bill not yet decided except a deterministic sample (`sampleSelection`, seeded by the cycle id) that stays pending for `review.decide` (basis `sample`); `review.queue` is the exception-based list (high first). Leaving `review` forward is refused while any bill lacks an approval (a hold counts as not approved). Decisions are `cycle_review` rows tied to the outcome id, so a new outcome needs a new decision.
- Events: `billing.cycle_opened`, `.cycle_state_changed`, `.cycle_closed`, `.cycle_outcome_recorded`. Audit: `billing.cycle_open`, `.cycle_move`, `.cycle_close`, `.cycle_outcome`, `.cycle_bulk_approve`, `.cycle_review`.

### Gaps
- Cycle procedures are not route-scoped (K-03): a limited member with `billing:run` can open/advance any route's cycle; account lists are filtered.
- Outcomes are created by P5-4's per-account job (below); `recordOutcome` by hand still works.
- A frequency of bimonthly/quarterly only widens the lock; there is no schedule generating cycles from it.
- Only `issueUsage` honours the lock; Accounts-driven documents (deposits, transfers) are not cycle bills.
- Approval is by permission and thresholds, not a K-05 request.

## Billing runs (P5-4: B-01, B-05 to B-10, B-12, NF-11, NF-24)
Files: `bill-account.ts` (one account of one cycle: builds the engine input from published reads, posts through `issueUsage`, records the outcome), `bill-job.ts` (the job `billing.bill_account`, and the consumer `billing.bill_on_reading`), `cycle-enqueue.ts` (job payload, deterministic keys, which accounts to queue), `cycle-progress.ts` (`run`, `retry`, `progress`), `run-input.ts` (pure: Metering readings to engine history, current reading, meter change). Both the job and consumer are registered by `registerKernel`.

### Engine inputs
- `billing.policy` gained `estimateMethod` (median, trimmed_mean, same_month_last_year, last_actual, fixed_minimum, none), `estimateTrim`, `estimateFixedMinimum`, `noneAction` (hold, standing_only, skip), `outlierFactor` (3, 0 = off) and `usageInvoiceTypeCode` (Tariffs code, default `usage`); with `estimateFallbackUnits` (baseline fallback) they are the engine policy. Defaults keep the old behaviour. `usageInputSchema` gained optional `outcome`, `disposition` and `meterChange`; `issueUsage` takes an `ActionEnv` (the system may run it), has `{ quietHold }` and returns `skipped` (engine skip) and, on `issued`, `flags` and `estimate`. Accounts' `AccountStatusView` gained `categoryId`; Tariffs publishes `invoiceTypeIdByCode`.
- Per account the job reads: Accounts' `chargesApplyingTo` (status, customer, category), Metering's `readingsFor` of the cycle account's service point (valid actuals; history = cleared routine readings before the period, latest per month, spans derived with confirmed rollover and across a meter change as old span + new span), `attemptsFor` and `dispositionFor` for the cycle date when the period has no reading. Period = month of `read_at` in the organization's time zone.
- No reading and no visit: estimate (engine policy; `none` follows `noneAction`). A visit with an outcome other than `read_ok`: its mapped disposition (M-06). A reading that is flagged or awaiting approval: exception outcome, left to the reading queue (M-05). A meter change (reading on a different meter than the baseline) passes `meterChange` from the old meter's `final` and the new meter's `initial` reading (M-07); missing either is an exception.

### Outcome mapping
billed or billed_estimate (an estimate line) citing the document; engine `skipped`, STATUS_BLOCKED, baseline-only and nothing-to-bill are `excluded` with a reason; any other hold (NO_BASELINE, NO_HISTORY, OUTCOME_HOLD, OUTLIER_HOLD ...) is an `exception` outcome, which is the one queue item (`UNBILLED_ACCOUNT`, owner the person who started the run, else the cycle's opener); DUPLICATE_PERIOD cites the existing live invoice as billed. Engine flags (ZERO_CONSUMPTION, STANDING_ONLY, OUTLIER_HISTORY, NO_ESTIMATE) do not block: the outcome is `flagged` (B-16) and each flag is a `READING_EXCEPTION` item (key `billflag:<cycle>:<account>:<code>`). Without Metering licensed every account is an exception saying so (issue by hand with `invoices.issueUsage` and record the outcome); the cycle itself still opens and closes.

### Job rules
- `billing.bill_account` payload `{ cycleId, accountId, ownerUserId? }`, key `bill:<cycle>:<account>:<n>` with n the number of outcomes so far, so queueing twice is a no-op and a retry after a failure is a fresh job. The bill's idempotency key is `bill:<cycle>:<account>`; an account with a billed, estimated or excluded outcome is left alone; accounts take turns by the posting lock; a closed cycle is a no-op.
- An unexpected error rolls the account's transaction back, then (new transaction) records an exception outcome (owner, age) and rethrows so the runner retries (30 s, 60 s) and after attempt 3 raises its own `JOB_FAILURE`. A later success records `billed`, which resolves the exception. Siblings are separate jobs. Tested by killing a job mid-way.
- Queueing: advancing into `estimation` queues accounts whose newest visit for the cycle date found no dial; into `calculation`, every expected account with no outcome. `cycles.run` (billing:run) queues accounts with no outcome, `cycles.retry` also those whose newest outcome is an exception; both are idempotent. `cycles.progress` (billing:read): counts by outcome, flagged, jobs by status, and up to 50 failures with reason and exception id.
- B-01: `billing.bill_on_reading` (on `metering.reading_recorded`) queues the account's job when the `billing.cycle` trigger and the cycle's own trigger are both `on_reading`, the reading is routine, cleared (not flagged or blocked) and not an estimate, and an unclosed expected cycle for its month exists. Batch mode and flagged readings do nothing; approving a flagged reading later does not queue a bill (use `cycles.retry`).

### Gaps
- Estimates the job bills are not written back to Metering (`recordEstimate`), so the estimate-cap policy (M-09) does not see them; no published Metering write for it.
- Without Metering there is no reading source in the job.
- A held `DUPLICATE_PERIOD` is only recognised as billed for invoices ending in the period's last day.
- Dimensions on ledger entries carry service point and category only (no scheme or zone: not on a published read).
- Progress counts jobs through `kernel.job` with a key prefix scan; fine per run, not an index.
- Throughput: see the report of P5-4 (`test/bench.test.ts`, `BILLING_BENCH=1`); each job still reads settings, tariff months and entitlements itself.

## Disconnection, corrections and revenue cases (P5-5: B-09, B-12 to B-14, B-19)
Files: `disconnections.ts`, `corrections.ts`, `correction-policy.ts` (pure, table tested), `correction-needed.ts` (two consumers), `cases.ts`. Schema: `billing.revenue_case` (migrations `0044`, `0045`). Settings: `billing.corrections` (grace window, large deviation, B-13 templates), `billing.revenue_protection` (`backBillMaxMonths` 12, `backBillRequiresApproval` true). Approval kind `billing.correction` (one level, `billing:approve`). Exception types `CORRECTION_NEEDED`, `REVENUE_CASE`.

### Procedures
- `disconnections.disconnect`, `.reconnect` need `billing:run` AND `accounts:change_status` (`p.disconnect`). `corrections.preview` (read), `.request`, `.post` (billing:run). `cases.list`, `.get` (read), `.open`, `.addEvidence`, `.resolve`, `.requestBackBilling` (billing:run). All take client-minted ids and replay.

### Disconnection (B-12)
One command keyed by the disconnection id. In one transaction: (1) a metered account (Metering licensed and a customer meter installed, `currentMeterOf`) must give `dial`; Metering's `recordFinalReading` stores it as a `final` reading (id derived from the event id, so a retry returns the same reading); a reading the validation blocks (BELOW_LAST ...) is refused and everything rolls back (confirm a rollover with `rollover: true`); (2) `issueFinal` posts the month in full plus the turn-off fee once (key `final:<id>`); the final dial is recorded in each line's `calculation.finalDial`; (3) Accounts' `disconnect_service` runs as the actor with the same id (`runStatusCommand`). Any refusal (held bill, a second event for the same month = DUPLICATE_PERIOD, Accounts' state machine) rolls the transaction back: never half done, and a retry of the same id changes nothing. Without Metering (or without a meter) the caller gives `dial` and `readings` history, as `issueFinal` always did. A first-ever reading only sets the baseline: the account is disconnected with `finalBillOutcome: "baseline"` and no bill. Later charges follow Accounts' status table (C-04).
- Reconnection restarts from the newest live final bill's recorded `finalDial` and the engine's `reconnect`. NO reading is stored (so disconnect and reconnect in one month cannot produce two different readings). A moved dial (up, or down) still reconnects but opens an `illegal_consumption` case (id derived from the reconnection id, `data` has final and found dial and `movedBy`) and its exception; nothing is billed for the moved units, the next reading's span covers them. A negative move never produces negative consumption. An account with no final bill from Billing is refused (use `accounts.reconnectService`).

### Corrections (B-13, B-14)
`proposeCorrection` rebuilds the engine account from Metering's cleared routine readings (history of every period, `carry` derived from units) and the stored documents and runs `correctPeriod` for the requested period and every later one that has a reading (a corrected reading moves the later spans). One credit or debit note per affected period (key `correction:<id>:<period>`), `related_document_id` the period's live invoice, lines with `service_from`/`service_to`, line description and document note from the B-13 template, the working (`billedBefore`, `repriced`, `difference`, dials, units) in the line `calculation` for office users. Issued today, so it lands in the current open period; the original is never touched and the cycle lock is not involved. A second call finds no difference (`outcome: "nothing"`, resolves the exception). Needs Metering (readings); without it use credit/debit notes.
- Who: `correctionPath` (pure). Within the grace window (default: the original invoice's due date; `days_after_issue` or no due date falls back to issue date plus `days`) `billing:run` posts directly. After it, or when there is no original invoice, an approved `billing.correction` request (`corrections.request`, subject `correction:<account>:<period>`, amount the sum of absolute totals; the approver cannot be the requester; used once, its id is the idempotency id; a re-price to another amount is refused). A large deviation (percent of the original total or an absolute amount, `billing.corrections`) needs evidence (a file id or a note of 5+ characters) on either route.
- Triggers: consumers `billing.correction_needed_on_reading` (routine reading flagged CLOSED_PERIOD, or a correction/supersede of a reading whose period was billed) and `billing.correction_needed_on_void` (a billed reading voided) raise a `CORRECTION_NEEDED` exception (no owner: team queue, age from raise time; key `correction:<account>:<period>`) which `corrections.post` resolves. They never post by themselves.

### Cases (B-19)
`revenue_case` is the log: `opened` (kind illegal_consumption, terminated_but_consuming, unbilled_account, back_billing), `evidence` (note, file id, data), one `resolved` with a decision (no_action, false_positive, back_billed, penalty_applied, referred). Opening raises a `REVENUE_CASE` exception (owner the opener) that resolving closes. `back_billed` posts a debit note (`backbill:<case>`) with the lines given, each at most `backBillMaxMonths` months before the current month, and (setting) needs an approved `billing.correction` request for subject `correction:<account>:backbill:<case>` (`cases.requestBackBilling`) of exactly the lines' total. Nothing is raised automatically yet for terminated-but-consuming or unbilled accounts (see gaps).

### B-09
The status table decides (C-04): DISCONNECTED (consumption not billed), DORMANT and TERMINATED by default bill no consumption; the final bill ignores the table. Tested on the cycle job path: an account that was expected at the snapshot and then disconnected, made dormant or terminated is excluded with STATUS_BLOCKED and no document, and a cycle opened afterwards does not expect it.

### Gaps
- `billing.invoices.issueCharges` (one-off charges from an invoice type's line template) is not built: Tariffs publishes no read of `invoice_type_line` yet.
- The disconnection month must not be billed already (DUPLICATE_PERIOD refuses); a cycle bill followed by a mid-cycle disconnection needs a void first.
- Unmetered accounts without a meter need the dial and history from the caller; flat-rate tariffs are not priced.
- No automatic detection of terminated-but-consuming or unbilled accounts (cases are opened by people, or by a reconnection); no auto-proposal of corrections (only the exception).
- A debit correction has no due date; Collections will need one.
- Corrections need Metering licensed; revenue-case `penalty_applied` records the decision but posts no penalty.
- Reconnection history: after reconnect the next bill still spans from the last routine reading with a true-up (the final reading is not a routine baseline in `analyseReadings`).

## Statements, simulation and reports (P5-6: B-17, T-06, X-01, X-03)
Files: `reads.ts` (statement), `simulation.ts` + `simulation-core.ts`, `reports.ts`; the engine is in `kernel/report-*.ts` (see `packages/api/CLAUDE.md`).

### Statement (B-17)
`billing.statement` rows keep every old field and gain `lineType` (`invoice`, `payment`, `adjustment`, `credit_note`, `debit_note`, `transfer`, `reversal`, `correction`; pure `statementLineType` in shared-types; a `correction` is a note whose idempotency key starts `correction:`), `key` (document id, else transaction id), `linkedTo` (the row it offsets: a reversal's original, a note's cited invoice, a reversed transaction's document), `replacesDocumentId` (a re-bill's replacement) and `pair` (`{ pairId, role: original | offset, settled }`). A pair is `settled` when the original and its offsets net to zero; `collapsePairs(rows)` (shared-types, pure) drops settled pairs for the customer view, the office view shows all. A partial credit note is never collapsed. The running balance is the ledger's over the whole history, so it is the same in both views. Pairs are computed inside the returned window only.

### Tariff simulation (T-06): `billing.simulation.run`
`billing:read` plus `tariffs:read` or `tariffs:manage` (FORBIDDEN otherwise). Input: `draft { type, content }` (checked with Tariffs' published `validateTariffContent`; flat rate refused), a selection (`accountIds`, `routeId` through Accounts' `routeServicePointFacts`, `categoryId`; at least one; intersected with the viewer's scope), `cap` (200, max 1000), `spansPerAccount` (3), `topMovers` (10). Spans are Billing's own live (not reversed) invoice lines `USAGE`/`USAGE_EST` (units and months from the line, category from the ledger entry of that line); the current column prices each span's months with the tariff in force in each of those months (Tariffs' `categoryPricing`, `tariffVersionInForce` through `pricing()`), the draft column with the draft's bands for the whole span, both with the engine's `spanCharge` and the organization's tier scaling, rounded once per span, bigint. `billed` is what was actually charged, for reference. Spans with no category or no tariff in force are skipped from both columns and listed. Returns per-account and total deltas, `affected` count, `topMovers`, `truncated`. Writes nothing (a test compares document, ledger and exception counts).

### Reports (X-01)
`billing.register` (documents by month, type, status, invoice type, source, account), `billing.receivables_aging` (the balance carried by the newest debits; buckets 0-30, 31-60, 61-90, 90+; `asOf`; by zone, category, account), `billing.cycle_completeness`, `billing.unbilled_accounts`, `billing.estimates_share`, `billing.revenue_cases`, `billing.corrections` (corrections and back-billing notes). All `billing:read`, scoped to visible accounts, from ledger, document and cycle tables only.

### Gaps
