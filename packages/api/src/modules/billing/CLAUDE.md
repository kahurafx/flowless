# Billing module (`packages/api/src/modules/billing`)

Phase 4 P4-3 and P4-4: B-05, B-06, B-08 to B-12, B-18, C-04, C-06, C-08, C-11, T-03, T-04, NF-22. Billing maps the pure `@flowless/billing-engine` onto the `billing` schema (append-only documents and double-entry ledger, see `packages/db/CLAUDE.md`). It reads other modules only through their `index.ts` (Tariffs' `categoryPricing`, `tariffVersionInForce`, `chargeItemAmountInForce`, `allocateInvoiceNumber`) and never touches their tables.

## Procedures
`billingRouter` mounted at `billing`, from `procedures.ts`: `read` (`billing:read`), `issue` (`billing:run`), `adjust` (`billing:adjust`).
- `invoices.issueUsage` (issue): price a reading or estimate for an account and period, post the invoice (or credit note when the total is negative) and its ledger transaction, or hold it. `invoices.issueFinal` (issue, `final.ts`): the final bill on disconnection (B-12): the engine's `finalBill` (final units, the month's standing charge and the turn-off fee, which is the amount in force of `turnOffFeeChargeItemId`, cited on the fee line), posted as an invoice (or credit note) with `document.final_bill = true`; once per `eventKey` (idempotency key `final:<eventKey>`, a repeat returns the first, another account under it is CONFLICT); whatever the status table says, it is not held. With no earlier reading the dial only sets the baseline and no bill is made.
- `adjustments.request` (adjust) asks the K-05 approval `billing.adjustment` (default one level `billing:approve`, `approvals.billing.adjustment`); `creditNotes.issue`, `debitNotes.issue` (adjust) cite the approved request (`approvalId`: same account, kind and amount; used once, the idempotency key is `note:<approvalId>`).
- `documents.void`, `documents.rebill` (adjust: permission, reason and audit, no approval); reads `documents.get`, `documents.list`, `balance`, `statement`.
Every change audits (`billing.<kind>_issued`, `billing.document_voided`, `billing.bill_held`) and publishes `billing.document_issued`, `.document_reversed`, `.bill_held` in the same transaction.

## Files
`usage.ts` (input schema, `prepareUsage` which builds the engine account and prices without writing, `issueUsage`, `holdBill`), `mapping.ts` (pure: tariff months to engine tariffs, engine lines to rows, stored rows to engine docs), `post.ts` (the only writer of documents and ledger entries: number, document, lines, balanced entries, or the mirror of a reversed transaction), `reversal.ts` (void and re-bill), `notes.ts`, `reads.ts` (derived reads), `settings.ts` (`billing.policy`: tier scaling, estimate average, fallback, outlier ceiling, dial digits), `approvals.ts`, `events.ts`, `final.ts`, `ports.ts` (Billing's side of the Accounts and Tariffs ports, registered by `registerKernel` through `registerPorts()`), `scope.ts` (K-03 checks against Accounts' published reads).

## Rules in force
- Postings for one account take turns (`lockAccount`, an advisory transaction lock); an idempotency key replays the earlier document, any other request under it is CONFLICT. A ledger transaction's key is `<document key>:ledger`.
- One ledger pair per non-zero line: receivable against revenue (deposit line: `deposit_liability`, transfer: `transfer_clearing`), sides swapped for negative lines; a document with only zero lines posts no ledger transaction. A reversal mirrors the original entries (swapped sides, `reverses_transaction_id`).
- T-04: usage and true-up lines cite `tariff_version_id` (the first when a span crosses versions; all are in `calculation`), standing lines cite `charge_item_id` and `charge_item_amount_id`. Meter rent is added to the standing line's amount and its amount id is in `calculation.chargeItemAmountIds`.
- A void is refused for a reversal, an already voided document, or an invoice with live notes against it. A re-bill prices the replacement as if the original were void; if the engine holds it, nothing is voided. Its pair keys are `<key>:void` and `<key>:rebill`.
- A held bill (engine `BillException`) raises an exception (`UNBILLED_ACCOUNT`, `NO_BASELINE` for that code) owned by the person who ran it, deduped per account, period and code; age is derived from `raised_at`.

## Ports and Accounts/Tariffs integration (P4-4)
All registered in `ports.ts`; the three Accounts ports carry `requires: ["billing"]`, so an organization without Billing is never asked (it terminates with `acknowledgeNoLedger`, activates by checklist, and succession stays `pending_ledger`).
- Termination check (C-11): `{ finalBillIssued, balance }`: a live (not reversed) `final_bill` document exists, and the receivable balance from the ledger. A voided final bill no longer counts.
- Gate source `payment_threshold` (C-06): `connectionPosition` = invoiced (receivable postings of charge, credit, adjustment and deposit transactions, reversals netting out) against paid (receivable postings of payment transactions and their reversals); met when paid covers the configured percent (rounded up) or fixed amount of invoiced; unmet with "No connection invoice has been issued" while nothing is invoiced. The account's whole invoiced total stands for "the connection invoice": a NEW or PENDING_PAYMENT account is not charged usage, so it holds only one-off documents. A gate decided by a source cannot be ticked by hand.
- Balance transfer (C-08): `balanceTransfer` posts one sealed `transfer` ledger transaction of four entries (old account receivable against `transfer_clearing`, new account's the other way; a credit moves the other way), `source_ref` and key `transfer:<commandId>`, so a replay posts nothing; a zero balance posts nothing; both accounts are locked in id order.
- Closed period (T-03): `closedPeriodSource` = latest `period_to` of documents that are not reversals and not reversed; Tariffs refuses a change dated on or before it.
- `chargesApplying` (C-04): `prepareUsage` asks Accounts' `chargesApplyingTo(tx, accountId)`. Consumption not billed holds the bill as STATUS_BLOCKED (exception `UNBILLED_ACCOUNT`); recurring not applying zeroes the standing line (it still cites the charge amount, T-04). An account Accounts does not know is billed as active (Billing can be exercised on opaque ids). Credit and debit notes, voids and final bills ignore the status table (corrections must stay possible).
- Scope (K-03): every procedure has `ctx.viewer`; `scope.ts` calls `accountVisibleTo` / `visibleAccountIds` (Accounts' index) so a limited member gets NOT_FOUND for an account, document or statement outside their areas and routes, and `documents.list` without an account is filtered to the visible ones.
- Applications (C-05): Accounts publishes `recordApplicationInvoiced` / `recordApplicationPaid` (the office's `markInvoiced` / `markPaid` as the system, replay by id). Billing does NOT yet consume `accounts.application_stage_changed` to issue the one-off connection invoice: an application has no account until `convert`, documents belong to an account, and the membership class and parent are not on a published read. Gap for Phase 6/7: the office still gives the references by hand.

## Assumptions until Phase 5
- No Metering: the caller passes the reading history (`readings`, the latest actual before the period is the baseline), the category and the invoice type. Flat-rate tariffs, discounts, quarterly or annual recurrence and late-reading corrections are not priced yet.
- `recheckActivation` on payment is Phase 6.
