# Metering module (`packages/api/src/modules/metering`)

Phase 5 steps P5-1a (M-01 to M-05, M-07 data side, M-11) and P5-1b (M-06, M-08 to M-10, M-12 and the Accounts ports). Schema `metering` in `packages/db` (migrations `0038` to `0041`; see `packages/db/CLAUDE.md`). The module reads other modules only through their `index.ts` (Accounts' `servicePointOf`, `routeServicePointFacts`, `servicePointOwners`, `accountVisibleTo`, `visibleAccountIds`; Tariffs' `closedThrough`; the kernel's `routesById`, `areasById`).

## Procedures
`meteringRouter` mounted at `metering`, from `procedures.ts`: `meters` (`meters:read`), `register` (`meters:read` + `meters:manage`), `read` (`readings:read`), `capture` (+ `readings:capture`), `approve` (+ `readings:approve`). New permissions `meters:read` and `meters:manage` (owner, admin, manager through ALL; supervisor both; accountant, clerk, technician read). Default roles: technician captures, supervisor approves.
- `meters.list/get` (meters), `meters.register`, `meters.changeStatus` (register).
- `installations.list` (meters), `installations.install`, `.remove`, `.replace` (register).
- `readings.record` (capture), `.recordEstimate`, `.correct`, `.approve`, `.void` (approve), `.get`, `.list`, `.latestActual` (read).

### P5-1b procedures
- `attempts.record` (capture), `attempts.list` (read); `outcomes.mapping`, `misses.get` (read); `snapshots.take` (register), `snapshots.list/get/tasks` (read); `handovers.list` (read), `handovers.linkClosing` (approve); `bulk.schedule` (meters). `meters.register` takes `kind` (`customer`, `bulk`, `master`) and `dmaId` (bulk/master only); `meters.list` filters by `kind`.

## Rules in force
- Meter lifecycle (M-01): `METER_TRANSITIONS` in shared-types. `installed` exactly while there is an open installation: `changeStatus` refuses `installed`, `replaced` and any move out of `installed`; `install`, `remove` and `replace` do those. Every move is an append-only `meter_event` with a client-mintable id (same id and request replays, anything else CONFLICT, via `steps.ts`); a trigger refuses a status change without a matching event.
- Installations: opaque `service_point_id` (existence and account from Accounts' `servicePointOf`); one open installation per meter and per service point; closing sets `removed_at` once (column-level grant plus trigger).
- Readings (M-02): `reading` is insert-only. Status (valid, voided, superseded), `cleared` (may be billed automatically) and a confirmed rollover are rows of `reading_status`; the newest `seq` decides; every reading has a first row (deferred trigger). Previous reading, units and baseline are derived in `reads.ts` (never stored). A baseline is a valid, cleared, non-estimate reading of the same meter. A correction (`correct`) is a new reading with `replaces_reading_id` (unique) and the old one gets a `superseded` row; a void is a `voided` row.
- Client ids (M-04): reading `id` is minted by the device; the same id with the same meter, value, time, source, purpose and reader replays (`replayed: true`), otherwise CONFLICT.
- Validation (M-05, `validate.ts`, pure): flags BELOW_LAST, ROLLOVER_CANDIDATE, ZERO, ABOVE_RANGE, BELOW_RANGE, SAME_DAY_DUPLICATE (organization time zone), OUT_OF_SEQUENCE (back-dated above a later reading) block; ZERO_CONSUMPTION and CLOSED_PERIOD are advisory. Setting `metering.validation` (high factor 3 of the meter's own recent daily use, optional low factor and absolute ceiling, history intervals, rollover fraction). A blocking flag stores `cleared = false`, raises a `READING_EXCEPTION` (subject kind `reading`, key `reading:<id>`, owner the reader, area and route from the service point) and the reading is not a baseline until `approve`; `approve` and `void` resolve the exception. `initial` and `final` readings (meter change) skip the zero and range checks; estimates skip validation.
- Rollover (M-07): drop below the previous reading with wrapped consumption under `rolloverMaxFraction` of the dial range is a candidate; the technician may pass `rolloverConfirmed` at entry or a reviewer `approve({ rollover: true })`; units then count the wrap. A confirmation the arithmetic cannot support is not accepted (BELOW_LAST / BAD_REQUEST).
- Estimates (M-03): `recordEstimate` stores `source = 'estimate'` (`kind: "estimate"`), never validated, never a baseline. Estimation policy (which method, when) is Billing's / later.
- `replaceMeter` (M-07): in one step final reading on the old meter (`purpose = final`), close its installation, old meter `replaced`, install the new meter on the same service point with an `initial` reading, new meter `installed`; replays by `id`. Work Orders will call it later.
- Closed period (M-11): `closedThrough` (Tariffs' port, which Billing fills) says the last closed day; a reading with `read_at` on or before it is accepted, flagged CLOSED_PERIOD (advisory) and published with `inClosedPeriod: true` for Billing's correction document (B-14).
- Scope (K-03): each reading procedure checks the reading's account against `accountVisibleTo` after the change (NOT_FOUND rolls it back); lists filter to `visibleAccountIds`. Meters themselves are not account-scoped.
- Photos (M-04): `photoFileId` is a `kernel.file` id; the file subject kind `reading` (`files.ts`) lets the reader or an approver attach photos after the reading syncs.

## Outcomes, misses, snapshot, handovers (P5-1b)
- Outcomes (M-06, `outcomes.ts`): every visit is an insert-only `read_attempt` (client-minted id, replay by fingerprint and reader, else CONFLICT) with one of the engine's eight outcomes, condition tags (max 12), note, photo and optional `cycleDate`. `read_ok` carries the reading (recorded through `recordReading`, id derived from the attempt id when not given; the service point must have a meter); every other outcome refuses a reading (a check also ties `reading_id` to `read_ok`). Setting `metering.outcomes` holds only overrides over the engine's `DEFAULT_OUTCOME_MAPPING`; `dispositionFor(tx, outcome)` and `outcomeMapping(tx)` are published for Billing; the shared-types lists are kept equal to the engine's by a test (`policy.test.ts`). The disposition is not stored on the attempt: it is looked up with the setting in force, and published on `metering.reading_attempted`.
- Eligibility snapshot (M-08, `eligibility.ts`): `snapshots.take({ routeId, cycleDate })` fixes one `eligibility_snapshot` per route and cycle date (unique, insert-only with its rows; asking again returns the first, `replayed: true`). Row facts come from Accounts' `routeServicePointFacts` (C-04 `billsConsumption` through Accounts' own rules, service point connection status) and Metering's open installations; decision order in the pure `eligibilityOf`: `service_point_not_live` (PLANNED or DECOMMISSIONED), `consumption_not_billed`, `no_meter`, else `eligible`. Walking sequence (S) is null: Accounts holds none. Reading tasks are not stored: `snapshots.tasks` lists a snapshot's eligible rows with state `to_read`, `read` or `missed` derived from the visits naming that cycle date. Billing (P5-3) reads `snapshotFor(tx, routeId, cycleDate)`. Limited viewers (K-03) see only rows of visible accounts.
- Miss counter (M-09, `policy.ts`): `consecutiveMisses` = non-`read_ok` visits since the later of the last `read_ok` visit and the last valid non-estimate routine reading; derived by SQL on every call. Setting `metering.misses` (`revisitAfter` 2, `selfReadPromptAfter` 2, `escalateAfter` 4, 0 = off, `maxConsecutiveEstimates` null = no cap): when the count equals a threshold, once per run, a revisit and an escalation raise a `READING_EXCEPTION` (subject account, keys `miss:revisit:<sp>`, `miss:escalate:<sp>`, owner the reader) and publish `metering.miss_threshold_reached`; the prompt publishes `metering.self_read_prompt_due` (Communications consumes it later). Exceptions resolve when an actual routine reading arrives. Work Orders has no published way to raise a job, so the "revisit task" is the exception (gap). The estimate cap (S): `consecutiveEstimates` counts valid estimates after the last actual; reaching the cap raises `estimate-cap:<sp>` and publishes `metering.estimate_cap_reached`.
- Zero consumption (M-10): billed and flagged (`ZERO_CONSUMPTION`, advisory) as before; setting `metering.zero_consumption.streakAfter` (3, 0 = off): that many cleared routine readings in a row with zero units open a meter-check `READING_EXCEPTION` (subject meter, key `zero:<meter>`) and publish `metering.zero_consumption_streak`; a reading with units resolves it. The hook is `applyReadingPolicy`, called at the end of `recordReading`.
- Bulk and master meters (M-12, `bulk.ts`): `meter.kind` and `dma_id` (a customer meter has no DMA); they are installed on a service point (an Accounts service point at the inlet), read through the same procedures, and `metering.bulk_schedules` (per DMA `everyDays` from `startDate`) drives `bulk.schedule` (due or not, derived from the last actual reading). Not on snapshots, never billed.
- Handovers (C-08, C-10, `handover.ts`): `readingHandoverPort` records one insert-only `reading_handover` per Accounts command and service point that has a customer meter, links a cleared actual reading taken on the effective date (organization time zone) through `handover_closing`, otherwise leaves it pending (a `READING_EXCEPTION`, key `handover:<id>`, subject meter) until `handovers.linkClosing`. It returns `closingReadingPending` so Accounts' change-of-name payload says `closing_reading_pending: true` for as long as a reading is owed; pending is derived from the missing link row. Accounts' stored payload is not rewritten when the reading is linked later (gap).
- Ports (`ports.ts`, `registerPorts()` from `registerKernel`, all `requires: ["metering"]`): `initial_reading` gate (met when the account has an initial reading or any valid actual one), `active_metered_no_meter` data-quality source (service points that once had a customer meter and none now, still in use, via Accounts' `servicePointOwners`; an account that never had a meter is not found), the reading handover, and the consumer `metering.close_and_open_on_relocation` of `accounts.service_point_relocated` (removes the meter from the old service point with the final reading when the relocation's text is a whole number, to stock, and installs the same meter at the new one with the initial reading; without an initial reading it raises an "install a meter at the new service point" item). Ids derive from the Accounts command id, so replays change nothing. An unlicensed Metering is never asked (NF-31), tested.

## Assumptions
Dial values are whole numbers (bigint); decimal dials are not modelled. An account is stored on the reading as it was at reading time. `read_at` may not be more than ten minutes in the future. A service point may have only one meter at a time (a bulk meter sits on its own service point). A miss is any visit without `read_ok`, whatever its disposition. The estimate cap counts estimates the module is given (Billing's policy runner records them with `recordEstimate`).

## Gaps (after P5-1b)
- No walking sequence (S): Accounts holds none, so snapshot rows carry null.
- Revisit and meter-check "tasks" are exceptions, not Work Orders jobs (no published job creation); no Work Orders kinds are registered by Metering.
- The disposition on an attempt is looked up, not frozen at the time of the visit; a changed mapping changes how old attempts read.
- Accounts' stored `closing_reading_pending` is not updated when the closing reading is linked later; read `metering.handovers.list` for the live answer.
- `active_metered_no_meter` finds only service points that had a customer meter; it cannot find an account that should be metered and never was.
- The relocation reaction reads final/initial readings only when the relocation text is a whole number; other text is ignored.
- No web or mobile screens for attempts, snapshots or handovers yet.
